Round 3: one sidebar, two bars

Focused B4 · option

Unread on top. Read, snoozed, scheduled, requests and archived chats fold into dividers until you open them.

  • Top bar: search (⌘K), filter by network or label, new message
  • Bottom bar: account, network status, settings
  • Hover a chat to archive or snooze it; wake or restore it from its divider
  • Schedule a message from the clock in the composer

Strongest at inbox zero: the list is only what needs you. Weakest at reaching someone you just talked to, which takes a click or a search.

Open Focused

Simple B4 · default

The same app with a plain sidebar: every chat in one list, newest first, nothing else in the way.

  • No headers or dividers; pinned chats stay on top
  • Requests, Snoozed and Archived are under the filter button
  • Same top and bottom bars, hover actions and scheduling
  • For people who just want a normal chat list

Strongest at familiarity: it works like every other messaging app. Weakest at triage, since unread chats mix in with everything else.

Open Simple

Settings B4

The gear opens a settings panel. Sidebar layout is one setting under Appearance, so each person picks Focused or Simple.

  • Appearance: sidebar layout, message previews, network badges
  • Notifications, Networks, Agents, Devices and keys
  • The account menu opens the same panel at the right section
  • The layout choice is remembered on this device

New accounts start in Simple; Focused is one setting away for people who want triage.

Round 2: Crossroads with one sidebar

Switcher B1

The list title is the navigation. Click "Inbox" to switch views, or narrow to one network or label.

  • Calmest screen: just the list, the chat and its settings
  • One menu holds views, networks (with beta tags and health) and labels
  • Active network or label shows as a removable chip
  • Offline networks surface as a banner above the list

Strongest at calm and space. Weakest at discoverability: networks and labels are one click away instead of visible.

Open Switcher

Tabs B2

Views become tabs over the list, and networks and labels become one row of chips you can scroll.

  • Inbox, Unread, Mentions and Requests always visible; the rest under ⋯
  • Network chips show unread counts and an orange dot when offline
  • Tap a chip to filter; tap again to clear
  • Close to Telegram's folder tabs

Strongest at keeping everything visible in one column. Weakest at scale: with many networks the chip row scrolls.

Open Tabs

Top bar B3

Navigation moves into a bar across the top of the window, and the left column is only the chat list.

  • Every view is a visible tab with its count
  • Networks are an icon group: counts, offline dot, click to filter
  • Labels in a menu; account and settings under your avatar
  • Full-height chat list with the most rows visible

Strongest at showing everything at once without a sidebar. Weakest at width: the bar gets crowded on small windows.

Open Top bar

Round 1: three directions

Triage A

Superhuman for messages. A split inbox you clear with single keys, plus a command palette for everything else.

  • Tabs: Important, Other, Agents, Done
  • J/K to move, E done, H snooze, L label, D draft with Dots
  • ⌘K palette: every action, every chat, scoped to the open chat
  • Undo on every destructive action

Strongest at speed for heavy users who live on the keyboard. Weakest at per-chat settings, which hide behind the palette.

Open Triage

Crossroads B

The familiar three-pane layout, done cleanly. Every control is one click away and visible.

  • Sidebar of smart views, networks (with maturity and health) and labels
  • Hover a message to react or reply
  • Switch which network a message goes out on (Mom: WhatsApp or Signal)
  • Settings panel per chat: notifications, labels, and how much Dots can do in this chat

Strongest at discoverability and fine control, closest to Beeper. Weakest at calm: it's the busiest screen of the three.

Open Crossroads

Stream C

One bar runs the app. A single calm column where chats open in place, and the power lives in the query.

  • Filter chips: in:signal is:unread label:family, combined with free text
  • > for commands; save any query as a view
  • Agent approvals pinned at the top as "needs you"
  • Chats expand inline with a quick reply, so you never leave the list

Strongest at looking simple while staying powerful. Weakest at long conversations, since the inline thread is short.

Open Stream
