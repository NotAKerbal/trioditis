New: iMessage, through a Mac you keep on: install BlueBubbles Server on it, turn on its Private API to send, then connect it in Settings → Accounts with its address and password.
New: iMessage has text and files, replies, captions on files, tapbacks, editing, unsending, typing and read receipts. Without BlueBubbles' Private API, a Mac only receives.
New: iMessage reaches Macs on the public internet, such as through BlueBubbles' proxy. To use Macs on your home network or tailnet, list those ranges in IMESSAGE_MAC_NETWORKS.
