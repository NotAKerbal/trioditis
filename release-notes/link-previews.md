New: Links in messages show a preview card with the page's title, description and picture when the sender's app made a preview (WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, Messenger and others).
New: A link without a preview, such as one in a text message, shows a card naming where it goes, such as Google Slides, YouTube or GitHub.
New: Link previews stay private: they are sealed with the message, and nothing fetches the page to make one.
